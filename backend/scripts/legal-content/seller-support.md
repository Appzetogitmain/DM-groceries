### Frequently asked questions

**How long does store approval take?**
After you submit your application and documents, our team usually reviews them within 2–3 working days. We will contact you if anything is missing.

**Why is my shop not visible to customers?**
Check that:
- your account is approved
- your shop is switched **Open** on the Dashboard
- your location pin and delivery radius are correct
- your products are in stock and approved

**How do I add or edit products?**
Go to **Products → Add Product**. New products or changes may need admin approval before they show to customers.

**How do I manage orders?**
New orders appear on your Dashboard and in **Orders**, along with a notification. Accept and pack the order quickly. A delivery partner will be assigned to pick it up.

**How do returns work?**
Customers can request a return within the return window. You can review the request in **Returns**, approve or reject it, and complete the quality check when the item comes back to you.

**When do I get paid?**
Your earnings appear in **Earnings** and **Wallet** after an order is delivered and the return window has passed. You can request a withdrawal to your registered bank account from **Withdrawals**.

**How do I change my bank details?**
Update them in **Profile**. Make sure the account holder name matches your verified documents.

**I forgot my password.**
On the login page, tap **Forgot password** and follow the steps.
