## Seller Privacy Policy

**Last updated: {{DATE}}**

This Privacy Policy explains how {{COMPANY_NAME}} ("{{APP_NAME}}", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects the information of sellers and store owners ("you") who register on and use the {{APP_NAME}} Seller Panel.

### 1. Information we collect

**Registration and business details**
- **Personal details:** your name, email address, mobile number, date of birth and profile photo.
- **Store details:** shop name, shop address, location pin, delivery radius, store category and opening status.
- **Business and identity documents (for verification):** PAN card, Aadhaar card, ID proof, GST certificate, trade licence, FSSAI licence, business registration and any other documents you upload.
- **Bank details (for payouts):** account holder name, bank name, account number and IFSC code.

**Information created while you use the panel**
- **Product listings:** product names, images, prices, stock levels and variants.
- **Orders:** orders, returns, POS sales and order history.
- **Financial records:** earnings, commissions, wallet balance, withdrawal requests and transactions.
- **Support messages:** what you send us.
- **Device and usage data:** device type, browser, IP address, login activity and notification tokens.

### 2. How we use your information
- To verify your identity and business before approving your store.
- To list your store and products to customers who are within your delivery area.
- To send you orders and assign delivery partners to them.
- To calculate your earnings and commissions and to pay you through bank transfers.
- To send order alerts, OTPs and account notifications.
- To provide seller support and resolve disputes with customers.
- To prevent fraud, keep the marketplace trustworthy and enforce our policies.
- To meet tax, accounting and legal requirements, such as GST and TDS records.

### 3. Who we share your information with
- **Customers:** your shop name, address, location, ratings and product listings are shown publicly in the app.
- **Delivery partners:** your shop name, address, location and contact number, so they can pick up orders.
- **Service providers:** payment and payout partners, SMS and OTP providers, push notifications (Firebase), maps, and cloud hosting and storage.
- **Authorities:** tax authorities, law enforcement or regulators, when the law requires it.

We do **not** sell your personal information. Your identity documents and bank details are visible only to our authorised admin and verification team.

### 4. How long we keep it
We keep your information while your seller account is active. After your account is closed, we keep financial, tax and transaction records for as long as Indian law requires, usually up to 8 years. Other information is deleted or anonymised once we no longer need it.

### 5. Your rights
- **Update:** you can update your profile, store details and bank details in the Seller Panel.
- **Delete:** you can ask us to close your account and delete your data by contacting Support. Pending payouts and orders must be settled first.
- **Withdraw consent:** you can withdraw your consent at any time. Without the required information, we may not be able to keep your store active.

### 6. Security
We use encrypted connections, password-protected accounts and restricted access to keep your data safe. Please use a strong password and never share your login details or OTPs with anyone.

### 7. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above. Continuing to use the Seller Panel means you accept the updated policy.
