## Privacy Policy

**Last updated: {{DATE}}**

This Privacy Policy explains how {{COMPANY_NAME}} ("{{APP_NAME}}", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal information when you use the {{APP_NAME}} app and website to order groceries and other products. By using {{APP_NAME}}, you agree to this policy.

### 1. Information we collect

**Information you give us**
- **Account details:** your name, mobile number and, if you add it, your email address.
- **Delivery addresses:** the addresses you save, including the house or flat number, landmark and the location pin you set.
- **Order details:** what you order, order notes and any return or refund requests, including photos you upload.
- **Support conversations:** messages you send us through Help & Support.

**Information collected automatically**
- **Location:** with your permission, your device location. We use it to show stores that deliver to you and to find your delivery address.
- **Device and usage data:** device type, operating system, app version, IP address and how you use the app (pages viewed, searches, crash logs).
- **Notification token:** used to send you order updates by push notification.

**Payment information**
Online payments are handled by our payment partner, Razorpay. We do **not** store your card number, UPI PIN or net-banking password. We only receive the payment status and a transaction reference.

### 2. How we use your information
- To create and manage your account and log you in with a one-time password (OTP).
- To show nearby stores and products and to calculate delivery charges.
- To place, deliver and track your orders, including sharing them with the seller and the delivery partner.
- To process payments, refunds, returns and wallet credits.
- To send order updates, OTPs and service messages by SMS, push notification or email.
- To respond to your questions and complaints.
- To prevent fraud and misuse, and to keep the platform safe.
- To improve our app, products and services.
- To meet legal, tax and regulatory requirements.

### 3. Who we share your information with
We share only what each party needs to do its job:
- **Sellers (stores):** your name, order items and delivery details, so they can prepare your order.
- **Delivery partners:** your name, phone number, delivery address and location pin, so they can deliver your order.
- **Service providers:** payment processing (Razorpay), SMS and OTP delivery, push notifications (Firebase), maps and location services, cloud hosting and storage.
- **Legal authorities:** when required by law, court order or a government request, or to protect the rights and safety of our users and the public.

We do **not** sell your personal information.

### 4. How long we keep it
We keep your information while your account is active and for as long as needed to provide the service. After that, we keep it only as long as the law requires, for example for tax and accounting records, or to resolve disputes. After that it is deleted or anonymised.

### 5. Your choices and rights
- **Access and correction:** you can view and update your name, email and addresses in your Profile.
- **Location:** you can turn off location access in your device settings. You can still enter your address yourself.
- **Notifications:** you can turn off push notifications in your device settings.
- **Account deletion:** you can ask us to delete your account by contacting Support. Some records may be kept where the law requires it.
- **Withdrawing consent:** you can withdraw your consent at any time. Some features may then stop working.

### 6. Security
We protect your information with reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), OTP-based login and access controls. However, no system is 100% secure. Please keep your OTP private and never share it with anyone, including people who say they are from {{APP_NAME}}.

### 7. Children
{{APP_NAME}} is meant for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

### 8. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above. Continuing to use {{APP_NAME}} means you accept the updated policy.
