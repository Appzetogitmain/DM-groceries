## Delivery Partner Privacy Policy

**Last updated: {{DATE}}**

This Privacy Policy explains how {{COMPANY_NAME}} ("{{APP_NAME}}", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects the information of delivery partners ("you") who sign up for and use the {{APP_NAME}} Delivery app.

### 1. Information we collect

**Sign-up and verification details**
- **Personal details:** your name, mobile number, email address and profile photo.
- **Identity documents:** Aadhaar card, PAN card and driving licence, including the driving licence number.
- **Vehicle details:** vehicle type, vehicle number, model and colour.
- **Bank details (for payouts):** account holder name, bank name, account number and IFSC code.

**Information collected while you work**
- **Location:** your live location **while you are online or on an active delivery**. We use it to offer you nearby orders, guide pickups and drops, show customers your progress and calculate distance-based earnings.
- **Delivery activity:** orders you accept and complete, pickup and drop times, OTP confirmations and return pickups.
- **Earnings:** earnings, cash collected for Cash on Delivery orders, wallet balance and withdrawals.
- **Support messages:** chats and messages you send to support.
- **Device data:** device type, app version, IP address and notification tokens.

### 2. How we use your information
- To verify your identity, driving licence and vehicle before activating your account.
- To offer you delivery requests near your location.
- To share your live location with the customer and store during an active order.
- To calculate and pay your earnings and to track Cash on Delivery collections.
- To send you order alerts, OTPs and account notifications.
- To provide support, keep you and customers safe, and investigate complaints or incidents.
- To meet legal and tax requirements.

### 3. Who we share your information with
- **Customers:** your first name, photo, vehicle details, phone number and live location, during an active delivery only.
- **Sellers (stores):** your name, phone number and vehicle details, so they can hand over orders.
- **Service providers:** maps and location services, SMS and OTP providers, push notifications (Firebase), payout partners, and cloud hosting.
- **Authorities:** law enforcement or regulators, when the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

We do **not** sell your personal information. Your documents and bank details are visible only to our authorised admin and verification team.

### 4. Location: your control
- We collect location only while you are **online** or on an **active delivery**. Go offline when you are not working.
- You can change location permission in your device settings. However, you cannot receive or complete deliveries without location access.

### 5. How long we keep it
We keep your information while your account is active. After your account is closed, we keep earnings, payout and tax records for as long as Indian law requires. Delivery location history is kept only as long as needed for support, safety and dispute resolution, and is then deleted or anonymised.

### 6. Your rights
- **Update:** you can update your profile, vehicle details and bank details in the app. Some changes need to be re-verified.
- **Delete:** you can ask us to close your account and delete your data by contacting Support. Any cash you hold and pending payouts must be settled first.
- **Withdraw consent:** you can withdraw your consent at any time. Without the required information, we cannot keep your account active.

### 7. Security
We use encrypted connections, OTP login and restricted access to protect your information. Never share your login OTP or a customer's delivery OTP with anyone else.

### 8. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above. Continuing to use the app means you accept the updated policy.
