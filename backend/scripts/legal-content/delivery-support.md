### Frequently asked questions

**How long does account verification take?**
After you submit your documents (Aadhaar, PAN and driving licence) and vehicle details, our team usually reviews them within 1–2 working days.

**Why am I not getting orders?**
Check that:
- your account is approved
- you are switched **Online**
- location permission is set to **Allow**
- you have good internet
- you are close to active stores

**The customer is not answering or is not at the address.**
Call the customer from the app. If you still can't reach them, contact support from the order screen before leaving the location. Do not cancel the order on your own.

**The customer says they didn't get the delivery OTP.**
Ask them to check their SMS and in-app notifications. If the problem continues, contact support. Never mark an order as delivered without the correct OTP.

**How are my earnings calculated?**
Earnings depend on the base payout and the distance travelled for each order, plus any incentives. You can see the details for each order in **Earnings**.

**How do I deposit cash I collected on Cash on Delivery orders?**
Your cash in hand is shown in the app. Deposit it using the method shown there to keep your account in good standing.

**How do I withdraw my earnings?**
Make sure your bank details are added and verified in **Profile**, then request a withdrawal from your wallet.

**How do I update my vehicle or documents?**
Go to **Profile** and submit the new details. They will be re-verified before they are applied.
