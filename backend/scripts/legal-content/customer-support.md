### Frequently asked questions

**How do I track my order?**
Go to **Orders** and open your order to see its live status and the delivery partner's location.

**Can I cancel my order?**
You can cancel an order before the store starts preparing it. Open the order and tap **Cancel**. If you paid online, the refund is processed automatically.

**I received a wrong, damaged or missing item. What should I do?**
Open the order and request a return within the return window shown on the order. Add photos if you can. Once the return is approved and the item is picked up, your refund is processed.

**When will I get my refund?**
- **Wallet:** usually instant once the refund is approved.
- **Original payment method:** usually 5–7 working days, depending on your bank.

**Which payment methods can I use?**
UPI, cards, net banking and wallets through Razorpay, and Cash on Delivery where it's available.

**Why can't I see a store or product?**
Stores show only if they deliver to your selected address and are open right now. Check that your delivery address and location pin are correct.

**I didn't receive my OTP.**
Check that your mobile number is correct and that you have network coverage, then wait 30 seconds and tap **Resend OTP**.

**Someone called me asking for my OTP.**
Do not share it. {{APP_NAME}} will never ask for your OTP, card details or UPI PIN. Please report the call to us.
